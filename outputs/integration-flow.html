Concurrent-led · Orderful onboarding · Updated Oct 22 after the 10/20 working session

Shehadey Foods integration flow

How EDI documents will move between Shehadey's plants and their trading partners once Aptean is replaced. Shehadey's BizLink moves files to and from an SFTP server that Concurrent hosts. Concurrent's Tray.ai layer translates between those flat files and the Orderful API. Orderful handles X12, 997s, and partner connectivity.

Decided (Oct 13 and Oct 20): Costco is the pilot partner · new Orderful ISA IDs for all partners · Concurrent hosts the SFTP · Park City orders stay on direct download · ~45 partners, ~6-month rollout.

Costco: two order channels, three ASN (856) paths

Umpqua (Roseburg, OR)No WMS
  • Orders: Costco sends X12 850 directly (today via SPS) → Orderful → Tray writes the ERMS file
  • Invoice: ERMS 810 → Tray → Orderful
  • 856: no pallet/SSCC data from ERMS; automated ASN not possible yet — WebEDI or order-only file (open)
  • Volume: 2 products, about twice a week
Fresno (Producers)WMS
  • Orders: Park City VMI — Costco's 852 goes to Park City, Park City creates the 850, Shehadey downloads it directly (not EDI). Stays as-is.
  • 856: WMS has real pallet/SSCC data → automated ASN through Tray
Fairfield & Model (NV)No WMS
  • Orders: Park City VMI, direct download (same as Fresno)
  • 856: keyed manually in Orderful WebEDI from the pick sheet
  • WebEDI needs the 850 in Orderful — depends on ReposiTrak (Park City's EDI provider) sending a copy via AS2 (meeting pending)
  • Volume: Fairfield ~10 Costco locations, ~10 shipments a day

Why mixed WMS complicates this: one partner (Costco) needs three different ASN paths depending on the plant. Plants without a WMS can't produce pallet/SSCC data, so their ASNs are either manual (WebEDI) or unresolved (Umpqua). Manual keying at ~10 shipments a day is slow and error-prone, and Costco charges back for bad ASNs. WebEDI also needs each Park City 850 in Orderful, which today bypasses EDI entirely. Shehadey plans WMS for the remaining plants by end of next year, so the build should let a plant switch from manual to automated ASNs without remapping.
